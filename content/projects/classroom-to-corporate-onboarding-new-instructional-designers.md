tagline: A 30-day onboarding program that turns former teachers into confident
  corporate instructional designers.
summary: A 30-day onboarding program for teachers transitioning into corporate
  instructional design. Built end to end needs analysis, curriculum map, sample
  deliverable, and evaluation plan not just outlined.
problem: L&D teams increasingly hire former classroom teachers into
  instructional design roles for their strong pedagogical instincts — but
  standard onboarding assumes corporate context (business KPIs, authoring tools,
  working through subject-matter experts) that these hires haven't been exposed
  to. The result is a slow ramp-up that mistakes a translation gap for a skill
  gap, leaving genuinely capable new hires struggling to prove it in their first
  few months.
outcome: "Designed to cut time-to-first-independent-project from roughly 16
  weeks to roughly 8, with every capstone deliverable meeting the team's quality
  bar without major revision. Built with a full Kirkpatrick-based evaluation
  plan: reaction through business results, so impact could be verified against
  real data if deployed."
