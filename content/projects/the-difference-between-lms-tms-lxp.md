tagline: Three acronyms, three different jobs, pick based on what's actually
  broken, not which one sounds newest.
summary: A side-by-side breakdown of Learning Management Systems, Training
  Management Systems, and Learning Experience Platforms, what each one is
  actually built to do, where they overlap, and where people mix them up. Covers
  a direct comparison table, situational guidance for when to use each (or
  combine them), a quick decision framework, and two adjacent terms (LCMS, LRS)
  that tend to get thrown around without being understood. Ends with a condensed
  set of takeaways for anyone who needs the short version.
problem: "LMS, TMS, and LXP get used interchangeably in conversation, but they
  solve different problems — and that confusion leads to real decisions: buying
  the wrong platform, expecting an LMS to do discovery work it was never built
  for, or running instructor-led logistics through a spreadsheet because nobody
  realized a TMS was the missing piece. This guide exists to give people —
  training coordinators, L&D leads, anyone evaluating ed-tech stacks — a fast,
  accurate way to tell the systems apart and match the tool to the actual gap,
  instead of the acronym that's trending."
outcome: >-
  Correctly explain what an LMS, TMS, and LXP each do without conflating them

  Use the comparison table to identify which system fits their specific situation

  Recognize when they need more than one system working together (and which combination)

  Walk through the decision guide to avoid over-buying tooling they don't need yet

  Know what LCMS and LRS mean the next time those come up in a vendor conversation
