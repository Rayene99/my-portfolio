tagline: A step-by-step guide helping teachers transition into curriculum and
  instructional design careers.
summary: A 27-chapter handbook organized into 7 parts that walks classroom
  teachers through the full skill set needed to move into instructional and
  curriculum design roles. It progresses from foundational concepts (learning
  theories, ID models like ADDIE, SAM, and Backward Design, mapping teaching
  skills to ID competencies) through needs analysis and planning, curriculum
  mapping and unit design, instructional strategies (active learning,
  e-learning, blended/online design), assessment design (rubrics,
  formative/summative, data-driven revision), tools and production (authoring
  software, storyboarding, project management), and finally career practice
  (portfolio-building, freelancing, certifications). Each chapter is paired with
  quizzes to reinforce understanding.
problem: Many experienced classroom teachers want to move into instructional
  design or curriculum development roles — in corporate L&D, e-learning, or
  freelance consulting — but lack a structured bridge between classroom pedagogy
  and the specific vocabulary, models, and deliverables of the ID field (needs
  assessments, Bloom's-aligned objectives, storyboards, rubrics, ID portfolios).
  This handbook closes that gap by translating existing teaching expertise into
  recognized instructional design competencies and giving a concrete, sequential
  roadmap — including the practical job-search and freelancing steps — for
  making the career transition with confidence.
